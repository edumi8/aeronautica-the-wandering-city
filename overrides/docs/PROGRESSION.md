# Campaign progression

## Chapter 1 — Foundations

Create a protected settlement with food production, storage, beds and at least two villagers.

## Chapter 2 — Motion

Build the first Create workshop, automate basic materials and establish rotational power.

## Chapter 3 — Steam

Construct a steam engine, rail connection and central warehouse. Begin producing components for the aerial platform.

## Chapter 4 — Flight tests

Build a small Eureka airship and a separate Clockwork prototype. Complete controlled take-off, navigation and landing tests.

## Chapter 5 — Project Ascension

Construct a 40–60 block-wide platform with housing, workshop, warehouse, bridge and docking area. Convert it into one primary ship.

## Chapter 6 — The Wandering City

Move the city between three distant regions, establish ground outposts and make the city food- and maintenance-sufficient.

## Chapter 7 — Electrical age

Use Create: New Age to build the electrical infrastructure, advanced production lines and launch-site support systems.

## Chapter 8 — Space programme

Build an Ad Astra launch complex, reach orbit, establish a station and found a permanent off-world colony.

## Final completion conditions

- One stable mobile city
- At least ten residents
- A working aircraft dock and auxiliary craft
- Automated food and core mechanical components
- An orbital station
- One self-sustaining extraterrestrial outpost
