# Mobile-city operating rules

## Recommended first city

- Main footprint: 40x40 to 60x60 blocks
- One primary Valkyrien Skies/Eureka ship
- Up to 10 villagers during initial testing
- One or two auxiliary craft
- Minimal active mob farms

## Safe while moving

- Housing and decorative blocks
- Ordinary storage after compatibility testing
- Navigation bridge
- Moderate lighting
- Small passive systems

## Prefer while anchored

- Large Create processing lines
- Villager breeding or trading halls
- Mob farms
- High-throughput item movement
- Large redstone networks
- Launch-pad operations

## Avoid initially

- Multiple large ships physically connected by many joints
- Hundreds of active block entities
- Updating physics mods independently
- Converting the city without a fresh backup
- Treating a successful small test as proof that every modded machine is ship-safe
